The **P2E No. 13** project is part of a collaboration with **Groupe ETPO**, specialized in maritime and industrial works.  
It aims to scientifically model the **drilling** operation—a process used to fracture rock for pile installation—and develop a **calculation spreadsheet** to estimate drilling progress per bit drop.

### Main Objectives
1. Build upon results from P2E 2022 and generalize them to **all rock types**.  
2. Integrate key parameters: rock type, pile diameter, bit weight and drop height.  
3. Refine model accuracy using the **Hoek-Brown criterion** and an **energy-based approach**.  
4. Determine drilling efficiency per hour based on mechanical parameters and winch capabilities.  
5. Produce **comprehensive documentation** (report, user manual) to facilitate tool usage.

### Implementation
- **Theoretical analysis**: study of differential equations, soil modeling as a damped system, hypothesis validation.  
- **Spreadsheet development**: correction of equations from previous P2E, addition of parameters for Archimedes' thrust and viscosity, graphical solution to determine fall duration.  
- **Reality comparison**: application to site data (Saint-Nazaire, Concarneau) and comparison of results with company estimates.  
- **Project organization**: rigorous follow-up with regular meetings (tutor and company), task sharing, calendar updates via Trello and Google Drive.

### Conclusion
The project resulted in a **more precise and flexible tool**, capable of estimating bit progression for different geological contexts.  
It enables better anticipation of material and human needs on site.  
Some uncertainties remain due to incomplete experimental data, highlighting the importance of future coupling between modeling and real-site monitoring.

This work illustrates the application of **mechanical sciences and civil engineering** to the planning of complex maritime operations.
