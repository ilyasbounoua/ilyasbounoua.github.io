### Responsibilities
- Customer welcome and service, order taking and preparation
- Strict compliance with hygiene, safety and quality standards
- Teamwork to ensure fast and efficient service
- Stress management and adaptation in a high-volume environment

### Skills Developed
- Customer relations and communication
- Organization and responsiveness
- Team spirit and versatility
- Adherence to quality standards and procedures
