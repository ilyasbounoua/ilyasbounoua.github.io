During my **end-of-studies internship** at **Padoa**, which publishes a SaaS platform for occupational health and prevention services, I developed full-stack features within the product team in charge of the member and prospect lifecycle.

### Tasks and Achievements
1. Duplicate detection between entities through a shared company identifier.
2. Resolution and publication of a sector's social worker contact on two portals.
3. Verification and enrichment of prospects automatically created from administrative declarations.
4. Bulk revocation of a user's access across all of their member companies.
5. Optimization of the pipelines keeping the search index (ElasticSearch) in sync.

### Environment and Methods
- Event-driven monorepo, Redux store on the frontend, asynchronous data pipelines.
- Agile methodology: sprint rituals, rotating Scrum Master and on-call support roles.
- Daily pull request reviews, design alignment with Product Managers and designers, collective testing sessions.

### Skills Developed
- Designing and shipping features end to end, from scoping to production follow-up.
- Managing consistency between the database and the search index.
- Writing and defending technical plans before implementation.
- Communicating with product and design teams.
