# Academic Path — École Centrale de Nantes / UCLouvain

This document summarizes my engineering program (2023–2026): **DATASIM** specialization in 2nd year, international exchange semester (S8) in the **Computer Science Master's** at Université catholique de Louvain, then **INFOSI** option in 3rd year.  
Official documents for my specializations (DATASIM / INFOSI) are available via the links below.

---

## 1) DATASIM — 2nd Year  
**Domains**: signal and image processing, statistical modeling, numerical methods, and applied learning.

**Educational Objectives**
- Master fundamental concepts of signal and image processing (transforms, filtering, restoration).  
- Acquire statistical methods for modeling and inference on real-world data.  
- Apply supervised and unsupervised learning to classification and estimation problems.

**Notable Projects & Work**
- Implementation of practicals and applied projects in image processing (preprocessing, segmentation, evaluation).  
- Development of analysis and visualization tools for multidimensional datasets.  
- Writing technical reports and team presentations of results.

**Acquired Skills**
- Data preprocessing and analysis, ML experimentation pipelines, model validation and evaluation, technical communication.

## 2) Semester 8 — Exchange (UCLouvain)  
**Context**: academic mobility within a Computer Science Master's program, with enhanced focus on data, algorithms, and scientific computing.

**Educational Objectives**
- Deepen advanced methods in learning, data mining, and programming paradigms.  
- Tackle complex problem-solving approaches (optimization, declarative and constraint programming).  
- Strengthen scientific development practice (programming for numerical computation and simulation).

**Experience & Benefits**
- Immersion in an international environment fostering autonomy, adaptability, and work in English/French.  
- Exposure to master's level content and demanding projects (case studies, mini-projects).  
- Development of an academic network and awareness of applied research challenges.

## 3) INFOSI — 3rd Year  **and** OP PERFECT

### OP INFOSI — Disciplinary Option

**Domains**: information systems, databases, software architecture, information system security.

**Educational Objectives**
- Design and implement reliable and scalable information systems.  
- Master principles of data modeling, relational and NoSQL database design.  
- Integrate best practices in security, software quality, and deployment.

**Notable Projects & Work**
- Design of applications with client-server architecture and robust persistence.  
- Case studies on application security, access management, and data protection.  
- Delivery of complete deliverables: specifications, prototype, tests, and user documentation.

**Acquired Skills**
- Software architecture, database design, requirements engineering, testing & deployment, operational security.

### OP PERFECT — Professional Option

**Domains**: project management and steering, governance, program management, project direction, operational management.

**Structure & Key Units**
- CFOND (Consolidation of Fundamentals) — advanced project management / steering methods and tools.  
- PROJAV (Advanced Project Management) — strategic vision, change management, master plan and multi-project management.  
- PRODIR (Program and Project Direction) — financing, team management, contracting, budgeting.  
- PROJPERF (Project Steering) — real-world project with professional client, continuous practical application throughout the year.

**Educational Objectives**
- Understand project governance and associated roles; plan, estimate, and track progress.  
- Manage risks, ensure budgetary follow-up, and manage project teams.  
- Build and carry a strategic vision (master plan), distinguish projects/programs/portfolios.  
- Implement a real project with an identified client, apply studied concepts in professional conditions.

**Notable Projects & Work**
- Execution of a client project throughout the year (steering, deliverables, communication, closure).  
- Practical exercises: GANTT, workload estimation, risk matrices, performance indicators, negotiation and contracting.

**Acquired Skills**
- Governance and steering of complex projects, program direction, budgeting and financial argumentation, team management and change management, institutional communication and client relations.
